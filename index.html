Settings

Appearance
Applies everywhere: the library and inside books.
Keep your library safe
Connect a folder and Backchat writes your books and notes into real files you own, then reads them back on any device that opens the same folder. The folder is the source of truth; the browser is only a cache in front of it.
No folder yet? The Save and Load buttons on the home screen keep a single backup file of everything, which you can restore from anytime.
Sync between devices
Keeps your marks, comments, margins and reading position the same on every device, through a small relay you own (a free Cloudflare Worker). Everything is encrypted on this device before it leaves, so the relay only ever holds scrambled data. Books themselves do not travel: add each EPUB once per device.
Setting up the relay for the first time? Make a token and Backchat copies it for you to paste into Cloudflare.
Encrypts your notes before upload. It never leaves this device, and nobody can recover it for you. Forgetting it loses nothing on your devices; you would just set a new one everywhere.
Send the link to yourself and open it on the other device: the address and token fill in, and you type only the passphrase. Treat the link like a password.
Turning off keeps everything already here. Your other devices keep syncing with each other.
Re-creates "How to talk back to your books", the built-in manual, with its sample marks.
Back up and restore
Files you can bring back. Use Save on the home screen for a full backup; Restore below reads any backup file: a full library, one book, a deleted book, or notes only.
Highlights, comments and progress only, no book files. With a folder connected it writes to a notes/ folder and keeps latest.json current; otherwise it downloads.
Pick any Backchat backup: a full library, one book, a deleted book, or a notes-only file. Merges by id, newest edit wins, nothing is ever deleted.
Export a readable copy
A readable snapshot of your reading as Markdown, one file per book, with your highlights, comments and the links between them. Made for your notes app, not for restoring.
Unzip it straight into an Obsidian vault. Connections become [[wikilinks]] so your books link to one another. A snapshot, not a restore point.
Maintenance
Optional housekeeping.
Lets you search inside book text. Runs once per book.
Compares the connected folder with this library: notes without a book file, book files not yet imported, hand-offs waiting to merge.
Deletes internal files from older builds and their cloud conflict copies. This build never reads them. Books, notes records and tombstones are not touched.
Folds any cloud conflict copies of your notes back into the real record, keeping the newest version of every mark, then removes the copies. Nothing is deleted until its marks are safely merged.
 
Danger zone
Irreversible. Make sure you have a backup first.
Erases every book, highlight, comment and reading position stored in this browser. Irreversible. Your connected folder and the files in it are not deleted; this browser is disconnected so the cleared data cannot sync back in.
